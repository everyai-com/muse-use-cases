02

Start here: the Chief of Staff prompt

If you're trying out @muse, @bot or any other personal assistant, the best first move is to connect the accounts you want it to reach — calendar, and email too if you yolo like me. Then paste this. It sets up the entire working relationship: what it learns, what it remembers, and what it must ask you before doing.

Chief of Staff Onboarding · memory · weekly review 
Loading…
Download (.txt)

What it does first

Checks which integrations are connected, reads the last 90 days of mail and calendar, then briefs you on what it found — separating facts, guesses and gaps.

How it learns

Keeps durable memory of your people, preferences and delegation rules, and runs a weekly self-review so it stops repeating the same miss.

Where it stops

No messages, events or commitments on your behalf during onboarding. Afterwards it acts inside the boundaries you two agree.

04

Taking one case instead

You don't have to hand over the whole pack. Every card carries the exact prompt that ran the job — copy it, swap the [bracketed] details for yours, send it.

  1. Filter to the job you want done — or search for it.
  2. Hit Copy prompt on the card.
  3. Replace anything in [square brackets].
  4. Paste it in. Muse asks for what's still missing, then runs it.
